Privacy and pilot terms

Plain-language boundaries for a short manual research pilot.

Independent status

Oregon Backflow Match Pilot is independently operated. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated for Oregon Health Authority, the State of Oregon, a city, or a water utility.

What buyers provide

The buyer form asks for a name, contact details, Oregon city or ZIP code, utility if known, property type, work needed, deadline, preferred contact method, and permission choice. A full address is not required at intake.

What providers provide

The provider form asks for business contact details, certification and license numbers, service area, work types, utility reporting capability, availability, response time, pricing preference, and contact permission.

Public provider research

The pilot may also record provider information available from public government lists, licensing lookups, and business websites. A public listing does not by itself establish current availability, utility acceptance, or endorsement.

How information is used

Information is used to assess pilot demand, check whether a request and provider appear to fit, make an introduction when possible, follow up on outcomes, and decide whether the pilot should continue.

Sharing choice

The buyer form asks whether contact details and the request may be shared with plausible matched providers. If the buyer says no, the pilot may send public-source options without forwarding the buyer's contact details.

Do not submit sensitive information

Do not submit utility account numbers, passwords, payment-card information, government identifiers, medical information, or other sensitive records. Contact your utility directly for an urgent compliance, safety, or service issue.

No guarantee or professional advice

The pilot does not certify, approve, recommend, employ, insure, or guarantee providers. It does not provide legal, regulatory, plumbing, engineering, or emergency advice. Confirm current requirements with your utility and verify the provider before hiring.

Pilot period and retention review

This is a 14-day validation pilot. At the decision review, the operator will assess which records are necessary for follow-up, opt-out suppression, evidence, and closure. No information will be sold during this pilot.

Contact and opt-out

To correct information, withdraw from the pilot, or request no further contact, email hello@kingbee.ai.
